Welcome to my personal portfolio – a single‑page web experience where the background itself comes alive!  
Time‑aware skies, a **fire‑breathing dragon** that swoops across the canvas, dynamic weather, falling meteors, and a fully glassmorphic UI. All crafted with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. 🚀

---

## 🔥 Highlights

- **🕒 Auto‑Switching Background** – The sky changes based on your local time: sunrise, day, sunset, or night. No page reload needed.
- **🐲 Flying Dragon** – A procedural dragon glides across the screen, flaps its wings, and **breathes fire** – even sets trees alight!
- **🌦️ Interactive Weather Control** – Switch between Clear, Rain, Cloudy, Windy, and Foggy – rain drops, clouds, wind lines, and fog particles are all simulated in real‑time.
- **☄️ Meteor Showers** – Toggle realistic meteors that streak across the sky and explode on the ground.
- **🌙 Dynamic Theme & Glassmorphism** – Night/sunset automatically enables a dark UI theme with glassmorphism cards, smooth transitions, and neon glows.
- **📱 Fully Responsive** – Looks great on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Hamburger menu for small screens.
- **💾 Single HTML File** – No build tools, no frameworks. Just open and run.
